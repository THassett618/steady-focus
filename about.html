About Steady Focus Co.

Steady Focus Co. sells small printable tools for planning, starting tasks and keeping track of time.

What we make

Three PDF tool sets, each $7: the ADHD Focus Bundle, the ADHD Task-Initiation Starter Kit and the ADHD Time-Blindness Toolkit.

How we decided what to make

We looked at what people say about planners and tools they bought or tried, and at advice written for people with ADHD. We looked for the same complaint coming up again: planners that are too big to maintain, lists that name the goal but not the first step, and schedules that assume you can feel time passing. Then we made small pages aimed at those gaps.

What we do not do

  • We do not give medical advice. These are organising tools and they are not a replacement for support from a clinician.
  • We do not show customer reviews, because we have none yet. When we have real ones, they will be real and they will say where they came from.
  • We do not promise results. Paper tools suit some people and not others. Our product pages say who each one is not for.
  • We do not run down other products. Our comparison guide says plainly that we sell one item on it.
